Who We Are

Real Estate With Purpose

“We don't simply see property as land or buildings. We see opportunities to build value, create assets, and shape the future.”

Akram and Ashraf Nigeria Limited is a Nigerian real estate company focused on creating value through property acquisition, development, investment, land sales, and property management.

We believe real estate is more than buying and selling. It is about identifying opportunities, making informed decisions, and creating valuable assets for individuals, families, businesses, investors and partners.

Our approach is built around integrity, professionalism, careful consideration and a clear understanding of client objectives.

Our Mission

Reliable. Value-Driven. Client-Focused.

To provide reliable and value-driven real estate solutions that help individuals, businesses, and investors acquire, develop, manage, and invest in quality property assets.

Our Vision

A Trusted Real Estate Company in Nigeria

To become a trusted and respected real estate company in Nigeria, recognised for integrity, quality, sustainable development, and long-term value creation.
